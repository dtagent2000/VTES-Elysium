# RULINGS — the register (started 20 Oct 2026; numbers are never reused or renumbered)

One line each, by mechanism. **R-n** = what the game says (rulebook, card text, a ruling, the owner, main). **D-n** = what OUR bot
does and why the owner chose it. **O-n** = waiting for the owner, the sources consulted in brackets. An answered O line moves up as
an R or D line; its number is retired. A line holds the rule in our own words and by role (D-141): who decided (the owner, or
Claude under his go-ahead, said so), not when or how it was built (the CHANGELOG). The owner's verbatim words are in the private
data repo's RULINGS-WORDS.md under the line's number; every line as it stood on 27 Nov, with its build story and sources, is in
`history/RULINGS-2026-11-27.md` (D-145). "CL n" = the old bootstrap's debt n (the data repo's `bot-history/CLAUDE-v2-2026-10-20.md`);
"review" = `reviews/REVIEW-playtest-2026-10-14.md`. Older docs say shark = expert, grinder = experienced. A superseded line says so.

## R — rules readings

### Bleed & bounce
- **R-1** (2026-09-16, the owner) — Block step: once blocks are declined only reactions remain (reduce, bounce); a Methuselah may try other minions until "No block", then the block step is over.
- **R-2** (2026-09-18, rulebook §4 + the owner) — One bleed and one political action per minion per turn; never the same named action card or card-in-play action twice; Freak Drive unlocks are otherwise unlimited.
- **R-3** (2026-09-20, the owner) — A NEW reaction window opens whenever a bleed modifier is played, so the bounce may always come after the modifiers; experienced players pump after blocks are declined and bounce on the total.
- **R-4** (2026-09-20, rulebook) — A failed block attempt does not lock the vampire; it may bounce in the reopened window.
- **R-5** (2026-10-16, Deflection / Redirection / Misdirection text) — A bounce's new target may attempt to block; the actor opens its block step in whatever window the bounce lands.
- **R-6** (2026-10-16, review F15) — A reduced bleed stays reduced on both sides; a bleed at 0 is unsuccessful: no Edge, no pool.
- **R-7** (2026-10-16, KRCG text) — The right to block follows the played MODE: a directed action only by its target (the client's `Target:` suffix, else the actor's prey); undirected by prey and predator; a non-target seat is silent.
- **R-8** (2026-10-19, card text) — Spying Mission [OBF]: only on a bleed that would succeed; the bleed burns no pool and is unsuccessful; the card goes on the vampire, +2 the next time it would successfully bleed the SAME Methuselah.

### Stealth, intercept & block
- **R-9** (2026-09-18, rulebook + the owner) — Rescuing my OWN vampire: +1 stealth, undirected; another's: 0 stealth, directed; the 2 blood may be split freely between rescuer and rescued. (The bot's own rescue keeps ≥ 1 on the rescuer: Claude's policy.)
- **R-10** (2026-09-18, rulebook + the owner) — Leave torpor blocked: the action fails, no combat, no cost; a VAMPIRE blocker may diablerise, an ally cannot.
- **R-11** (2026-09-20, card text) — Second Tradition: Domain has a second mode (a LOCKED prince or justicar burns 1 blood to unlock and block at +2 intercept) and REQUIRES a title; an untitled play is illegal.
- **R-12** (2026-09-24, the owner on the base rules) — Stealth and intercept are spent only when needed, bot-vs-bot too: stealth only after a block attempt and only while intercept ≥ stealth; intercept only while stealth > intercept.
- **R-13** (2026-09-24, the owner) — Superior Govern the Unaligned announced at stealth 1 is right.
- **R-14** (2026-09-25, rulebook "Action cards") — Directed actions carry 0 stealth; the undirected +1 applies to BASIC actions only (hunt, equip, employ, recruit, political, rescue own, leave torpor); an action card is at 0 unless it prints stealth, and "+N stealth action" is the total (`actStealth`).
- **R-15** (2026-09-25, vtes-rulings) — Bowl of Convergence [AUS] is a PAID +1 intercept; a minion committed to a gun's strike by its maneuver gets no strike under Immortal Grapple.
- **R-16** (2026-09-22, CL 71) — Intercept is per minion: a human's intercept typed while another minion's attempt is live books onto the CURRENT attempt.
- **R-38** (2026-10-17, rulebook + the owner via main) — Stealth and intercept can go below 0 (a bare Christine Boscacci blocks at −1); the table keeps the sign (block mode only; votes and damage stop at 0).

- **R-19** (2026-09-18, rulebook) — Aggravated damage on a ready vampire: the first point sends it to torpor, every further point burns 1 blood or the vampire when it has none (Daring the Dawn [for] at 0 blood burns it).

- **R-23** (2026-10-10, rulebook) — A steal takes at most what the victim's vampire holds and resolves at its RULES moment (after my strike), so my dodge or combat ends stops it.

- **R-27** (2026-10-16, KRCG) — Immortal Grapple [pot]: non-hand strikes are barred for EITHER combatant this round; the restriction dies with the round.
- **R-28** (2026-10-16, rulebook 6.4.3 + the owner) — The acting minion declares its strike first; between bots the answering bot peeks at it before choosing.
- **R-29** (2026-10-16, Deep Song text) — The rushed vampire is the acting minion in that combat: it speaks first in the handshake and the human phase walk.

- **R-39** (2026-10-29, rulebook, card texts, rulings; `specs/spec-v5-wave1-2026-10-29.md` §1) — Additional strikes follow the completed first pair, the acting minion deciding first and the impulse returning; one limited source per minion a round (Quickness [CEL] outside it, one Quickness a round); a combatant no longer ready ends the combat. Kevlar Vest and Guardian Angel prevent aggravated damage too; Claws of the Dead covers the round's additional hand strikes; Invigorate's combat effects last the blocked action; Wall of Filth is prevention, not a strike.
- **R-41** (2026-10-29, rulebook, card texts, rulings; `history/specs/spec-v5-wave2-2026-10-29.md` §1) — Maneuvers go back and forth, the acting minion first, until one combatant lets the range stand; a strike card's optional maneuver commits the round's strike [LSJ 20021028], no second strike card's or weapon's maneuver follows it that round, and a committed hand strike the range takes away is no strike at all (by analogy with [TOM 19951217]); a bundled optional press is usable only the round its card was played [TOM 19960521] (Form of the Wolf [PRO]'s 'this combat' by that card's ruling); Soul Burn's damage cannot be prevented by cards requiring Fortitude [LSJ 20050628], and its opposing-weapons rider holds, but not against a weapon strike with first strike unless Soul Burn has first strike too [LSJ 20040928]; Torn Signpost sets the base strength [PIB 20130303] and +X strength adds to it [LSJ 20100813]; Hunger of Marduk's provided strike can be used more than once in its round [ANK 20220207]; 'not usable during the first round' means from round 2.

### Bleed & bounce
- **D-1** (2026-09-15, the owner) — "Will this action cause me to lose pool?" is answered per current action (cleared at It resolves or the next action); unanswered = the wait's clock (tempo kind `pq`; askSecs only with no botcfg), default NOT blocking.
- **D-2** (2026-09-20, the owner's defence doctrine) — Against a stealth bleeder the defender CLOGS: no attempt on its 1-bleeds (the stealth stays stuck in its hand) while the pool can afford the pump seen; a front-heavy bleed (a Govern) gets an attempt so it must spend stealth, better still with a bounce after; clogging is a gamble one eventually cannot afford. (The pool test, pool > the critical level + the biggest pump seen, is Claude's.)
- **D-3** (2026-09-20, the owner) — Wake + block / bounce only on bleeds that END at 2+ after the modifiers; the bounce is played on the TOTAL, never at the announce (bouncing a 1 at once is a beginner's mistake); a bounced bleed is very rarely pumped. The bot still bounces a total of 1 at the pool's edge and when a spare bounce stays in hand (Claude's synthesis, dynamic by D-5; its parameters: O-1).
- **D-4** (2026-09-20, the owner) — The bleed total is asked before the bleed resolves. As TARGET of a human actor the bot says "Any bleed modifiers?" and bounces on the total at the actor's word or the clock (D-136; `lateBounce`). As ACTOR it should open the total window to a HUMAN target too (the total decides a lot, and it is how humans play each other): behind `askHumansTotal`, off until the suite's human harness learns the second question (O-2). Quick phrases in a reactions-only ask: No = no reaction, Yes = wait.
- **D-5** (2026-09-21, the owner) — The bounce track first, with a DYNAMIC spare-bounce reserve (`_bounceReserve`); Nosferatu waits.
- **D-6** (2026-09-21, the owner) — Splitting the pump over two actions is good above all when a bounce is expected (less lost to the bounce, at the cost of stealth on two actions and of the full Govern + Conditioning stack): a comparison of ODDS about my deck and the opponent's. Not built. (Making it the abstract model's first consumer is Claude's plan, not the owner's ruling.)
- **D-7** (2026-10-16, the owner) — A human's reduction is read from the client's `NAME reduces the bleed by N.` Reduce row (the owner's interim; curated reduce cards later). Setting the target is player education, not forced by the flow: with no Target suffix a directed action counts as aimed at the actor's prey (R-7); the guide says to set it first.

### Stealth, intercept & block
- **D-8** (2026-09-11, the owner) — Bots block undirected actions; the hard part is prioritising which (`specs/elysium-bot-block-doctrine.md`).
- **D-9** (2026-09-17, the owner) — Bare-block defaults novice off / experienced chump / expert any; desperation overrides; thresholds come from a per-minion pool-damage forecast and a combat EV, never constants.
- **D-10** (2026-09-18, the owner) — Hold at most 3 stealth cards, preferably different ones (`_stealthGlutIndex`); a wake in hand counts as a body at home. (A 4th when my prey blocks ≥ 40 % of my bleeds is Claude's extension.)
- **D-11** (2026-09-20, the owner's stealth-bleed doctrine) — Every deck, aggressive ones above all, must gamble: 2 stealth = decent, 3 + a pump = really good, 1 = a gamble, a replacement draw is always a chance; contest a block by the stealth STACK in hand against the intercept the blocker can plausibly add; drain the defender's wakes and intercept until two bleeds TOTALLING ~12 land; all nuanced by the opponent's statistics.
- **D-12** (2026-09-20, the owner's probe rule) — A bleed of 1 is RESERVED for making the best conditions for a stronger action when I hold NO stealth (perhaps 1 when worried); a Govern without stealth while the prey has a body up is held, played at superior, or preceded by a chump bleed from ANOTHER vampire (a chump bleeder preferably with a combat ends / dodge / maneuver in hand).
- **D-13** (2026-09-20, the owner) — Playing ONE intercept or stealth card when more are held and needed is a bug: the SECOND LOOK stacks on both sides.
- **D-14** (2026-09-22, the owner) — With stealth > intercept on the counter the actor plays no stealth and says `currently has N stealth`; while contested a bare `N` in chat = the blocker's total intercept, `+N` = added.
- **D-15** (2026-09-25, the owner's question, Claude's design) — Stealth by chat while a bot blocks a human: the actor's typed `N` / `+N` books their stealth; the client's free counter is not read.
- **D-16** (2026-09-28, the owner) — The block doctrine (do not block Nosferatu or Tremere unless you must) and Malkavian's wave read `oppCombat` and became CALIBRATION (`blockCost`, `waveNarrow`).
- **D-17** (2026-10-16, the owner) — Probe then punch: the probers keep off the heavy card only when my actors OUT-NUMBER my prey's unlocked bodies (`probeHold` 2; 0 = the probe order without the reservation).
- **D-95** (2026-09-18, the owner) — Opponents' actions are classed offensive / economy / utility in the per-minion statistics (`_actionClass`); the hit rate divides blocked by offensive only.
