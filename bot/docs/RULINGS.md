One line each, dated, by mechanism. **R-n** = what the game says (rulebook, card text, the owner's ruling, a main reply). **D-n** =
what OUR bot does and why the owner chose it. **O-n** = waiting for the owner, with the sources consulted. An answered O line moves up
as an R or D line with its date; the O number is retired. "mem" = the owner's dated decision notes; "CL n" = the old CLAUDE.md debt n
(the private data repo's `bot-history/CLAUDE-v2-2026-10-20.md`); "review" = `reviews/REVIEW-playtest-2026-10-14.md`; the round specs
(spec-cards, spec-next, spec-r4), the journal and the context are in the data repo's `bot-history/` (D-142). Older docs say shark =
expert, grinder = experienced. Where a line was superseded, the current one stands and says so. A line says what the owner decided in
our own words and by role; the verbatim words are in the private data repo's RULINGS-WORDS.md under the line's number (D-141).

### Bleed & bounce
- **R-1** (2026-09-16, mem) — Block step: once blocks are declined only reactions remain (reduce, bounce); a Methuselah may try other minions until "No block", then the block step is over.
- **R-2** (2026-09-18, rulebook §4 + the owner, CL 26b) — One bleed and one political action per minion per turn; never the same named action card or card-in-play action twice; Freak Drive unlocks are otherwise unlimited (the owner).

- **R-7** (2026-10-16, KRCG text, review F1) — The right to block follows the played MODE: a directed action only by its target (the client's `Target:` suffix, else the actor's prey); undirected by prey and predator; a non-target seat is silent.
