# (22a, v0.6.152) Elysium Bots — delivery 22 September 2026

**Cumulative on top of 21c/21d (replaces nothing else — the 21 Sep files stay valid; this adds one release).**

**Johan's 1v1 report vs malkavian (22 Sep), two small bugs, one release:**

1. **THE BEAT ANCHOR.** `_pace()` waited paceMs since the bot's OWN last visible act, so a human's `No block` / `Block!` / tally arriving 6 s after the bot's `Block?` found the beat spent — the modifier, the reaction, the resolve fired in the same instant as the answer. The beat is now measured from the LATEST visible event at the table, mine or anybody's (`_lastTableAt`, stamped in `_dispatch` on say / log / chat / tally / fx from another sender; server ledger lines and board pushes never re-anchor). Defaults: **paceMs 2000 → 1500**, **reactPaceMs = paceMs** (was 0.6× — Johan: one breath everywhere). New helper `_afterBeat(kind, guard, fn)`: a synchronous handler lets a visible act land after the beat if the world still looks the same; paceMs 0 runs it at once, so the suite and the arena are byte-for-byte unchanged.
2. **THE STEALTH FIRST LOOK.** It ran in the shout's tick (before the client's 120 ms tally with a Sport Bike's intercept, before the human had played anything) and floored `need` to 1 — so a Govern at superior (counter 1-0) answered every Block! with a stealth card it did not need, and again when a new blocker zeroed b at 3-0. Now: the contest opens at once, the look waits the reaction beat and reads the counter as it then stands; while my stealth already beats their intercept the bot says **`<vamp> currently has N stealth — play your intercept, or resolve the block.`** (to a human blocker, once per blocker/state; trace `stealth-ahead`) and waits for the counter to move — the existing second look then answers on the real numbers. Second-look stealth, the blocker's intercept card and cancel-block land after the beat too, re-checked against the counter.
3. **INTERCEPT BY CHAT (Johan's design).** While my action is contested, the blocker's bare **`N`** = total intercept right now, **`+N`** = added since; the bot books it on the shared counter for them (`_setTallyB`), notes `X has N intercept (booked on the counter).`, judges the real counter at once. Only from the blocker or `@Name N`; outside a duel a bare number means nothing. `help` teaches it.
