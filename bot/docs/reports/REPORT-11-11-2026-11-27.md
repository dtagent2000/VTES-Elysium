## What was built
- **The rule** (the rulebook, who may attempt to block, and its detailed course of an action): an undirected action is the actor's
  prey's to block first and its predator's once the prey has declined; a minion may attempt again and again "as long as another minion
  is not already blocking"; an attempt is resolved once every Methuselah has passed. The bot read neither half at a human table.
- **Part 1, one attempt at a time.** `_standing`: another seat's attempt as the table showed it (its attempt line; a human's Block!
  shout too), until its verdict, a cancel, the counter leaving block mode, It resolves, my own announce or the turn's end. A bot's
  react window and its decision wait while one stands (`_standingFor`, against the pending's own announce, `annAt`); the acting bot
  takes up one attempt at a time (`a.attemptBy`; `_refuseAttempt`: not taken up, no blocker renamed, a `second-attempt` row, and a
  human told once: "Trainbot: <minion>’s block attempt is still standing — one block attempt at a time.").
- **Part 2, the predator after the prey.** The pool question's window (a human's action and "Any blocks?", a human's referendum) had
  no order: a not-directed action now gives the prey and the predator their roles, and the predator's window waits for the prey's
  decline (`_preyDeclined`: the quick phrases, the typed form, and a decline said before the window was born, `_declAt`); the prey's
  Pass inside its own standing attempt is no decline.
- **Part 3, a human blocker's Pass.** At a bot's undirected action the human blocker's Pass (said or typed) inside the human's own
  attempt resolves it -- the verdict from the counter, at once in Turn-based, at the clock otherwise -- and is no pass of the seat.
- **Part 4, the action's stealth.** A blocker's seed of the rulebook default reads a human actor's stealth as fresh against the
  action's own announce (`p.annAt`), not the short-term memory (`_obs`), which any later play line replaces: the second blocker at the
  playtest wrote a=0 over the action's +1.
- **4.25.** A human's quick "No" answers a block question (its prompt says `say "block" or "no"`; the typed "no" already did), and
  opens a bot predator's window at a bot actor's question (at a human actor's a "No" may answer a pool question).
- **11.12.** The turn-end note is silent for a record with nothing at stake and names the action otherwise ("Note: <who>’s action
  (<card>) was still unresolved when the turn ended, with N pool at stake for me — …", "vote N" for a referendum); a human's 0-pool
  undirected record closes at my own decline (`_closeZeroRecord`), so it no longer lingers to the turn's end or keeps me from the
  next "Any blocks?".
