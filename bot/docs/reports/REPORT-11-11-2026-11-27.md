## What was built
- **The rule** (the rulebook, who may attempt to block, and its detailed course of an action): an undirected action is the actor's
  prey's to block first and its predator's once the prey has declined; a minion may attempt again and again "as long as another minion
  is not already blocking"; an attempt is resolved once every Methuselah has passed. The bot read neither half at a human table.
- **Part 1, one attempt at a time.** `_standing`: another seat's attempt as the table showed it (its attempt line; a human's Block!
  shout too), until its verdict, a cancel, the counter leaving block mode, It resolves, another seat's next action row (not the menu
  row of the action whose card was just played), my own announce or the turn's end. A bot's react window and its decision wait while one stands (`_standingFor`: an attempt since the
  pending's own announce, `annAt`, by a seat that may block it -- an undirected action's prey or predator; never at a directed
  one), and open again when it ends with no verdict (`_standingEnded`). Only such a seat's attempt is recorded, each line stamps it
  afresh, and one older than the action it is at (`_actNow`: my record's, the pool question's, or a remembered action line; an
  action line newer than a lingering record overtakes it, `_newerAction`) is dropped. The acting bot takes up one attempt at a time (`a.attemptBy`; `_attemptStands`: the standing one by a seat that may block,
  `_mayBlockAt`, whose line takes a contest a stray attempt opened; `_refuseAttempt`: not taken up, no blocker renamed, a
  `second-attempt` row, and a human told once: "Trainbot: <minion>’s block attempt is still standing — one block attempt at a
  time.").
- **Part 2, the predator after the prey.** The pool question's window (a human's action and "Any blocks?", a human's referendum) had
  no order: a not-directed action now gives the prey and the predator their roles, and the predator's window waits for the prey's
  decline (`_preyDeclined`: the quick phrases, the typed form, and a decline said before the window was born, `_declAt`); the prey's
  Pass inside its own standing attempt is no decline, before the window's birth too (`_noteDecline` marks it, the typed form as the
  quick phrase). A record of a human's earlier action (no It resolves came) is superseded by the next "Any blocks?" when the
  remembered line is a new action (`_newerAction`), so a prey bot that kept one answers again -- not the record's own action's card
  (its minion's card after its row), nor while my block or my wait for the actor's word is under way; until then it takes no word
  on the new action, and a record with my pool at stake gets the turn-end note's text at once ("Note: an unresolved bleed at me was
  overtaken by <who>’s next action — if it landed, tell me "bleed N"."; pass 1's supersede had dropped it unsaid), whose phrase the
  next pool question lets through while it is owed (otherwise "bleed 2" at How much? is the answer 2, as before). Any line by the record's own acting minion is its own action (its card after its row, its row
  after its card; but an undirected menu row after its card still opens a record of its own: register 11.17), a card whose name holds a period is read from the whole line, and a typed "no block" while the pool question is
  open is a decline, not the actor's No (v0.6.110's reader took it from anyone).
- **Part 3, a human blocker's Pass.** At a bot's undirected action the human blocker's Pass (said or typed) inside the human's own
  attempt resolves it -- the verdict from the counter, at once in Turn-based, at the clock otherwise -- and is no pass of the seat.
- **Part 4, the action's stealth.** A blocker's seed of the rulebook default reads a human actor's stealth as fresh against the
  action's own announce (`p.annAt`), not the short-term memory (`_obs`), which any later play line replaces: the second blocker at the
  playtest wrote a=0 over the action's +1.
- **4.25.** A human's quick "No" answers a block question (its prompt says `say "block" or "no"`; the typed "no" already did), and
  opens a bot predator's window at a bot actor's question (at a human actor's a "No" may answer a pool question).
- **11.12.** The turn-end note is silent for a record with nothing at stake and names the action otherwise ("Note: <who>’s action
  (<card>) was still unresolved when the turn ended, with N pool at stake for me — …", "vote N" for a referendum, "@<bot> …" when
  the actor is not that bot's predator, as the damage grammar needs); a human's 0-pool
  undirected record closes at my own decline (`_closeZeroRecord`), so it no longer lingers to the turn's end or keeps me from the
  next "Any blocks?".
