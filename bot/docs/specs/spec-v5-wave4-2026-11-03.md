# V5 wave 4 — the masters on a vampire, and Toreador's last cards (3 Nov 2026, bot v0.6.217)

Johan's decision (3 Nov 2026, RULINGS D-106, Claude's recommendation taken): wave 4 = the eight masters that go on a vampire
(Oblivion, Charisma, Depravity, Terrifying Visage, Monster, Saulot's Avenging Fist, Saulot's Guiding Wisdom, Regent) + Toreador Grand
Ball + Toreador's three last cards (Scalpel Tongue, Change of Target, Toreador Justicar), which share the masters' mechanisms (title
cards and their votes, the 'if blocked' step, the polling-step abstain). With them every Toreador row can turn playable — the first V5
precon that could join the menu (D-98). The eighteen masters 'in play' and Meditative Grove's cancel are wave 5. Sudden Reversal (Johan,
3 Nov, D-107): an 'as played' window, also against humans; its shape (a silent bot-to-bot grace + the human's own Hold on, or a
collective bot Hold on with 'No reaction' / the card) is wave 5's design.
This page is the wave's canon: the readings with their sources (§1; R-43), the protocol (§2), the decisions (§3; D-106: Claude's
design, not Johan's rulings), what v1 leaves out (§4; BOARD), the tests and the registered prediction (§5).

## 1. The readings (KRCG's v5 text; rulings by their vtes-rulings references)
- **One title per vampire** (the rulebook, quoted on the VEKN forum, 'A question about titles'): a vampire cannot have more than one
  title; a vampire that gains a title loses the one it had, and a lost title's card burns. A title card on a vampire therefore REPLACES
  its printed title. Votes by title (the rulebook's tables, cardfx's crypt `votes`): Justicar 3, regent 4, a '2 votes' title 2.
- **Oblivion** (Discipline master): on a vampire — +1 level of Oblivion (none → [obl], [obl] → [OBL]) and +1 capacity; never on a
  vampire with [OBL].
- **Charisma** (unique): on a ready vampire — its recruit-ally ACTIONS cost 1 blood or pool less (Piper's recruit is no action and
  gets no discount [LSJ 20090115-1]).
- **Depravity** (unique, 1 pool): on a ready vampire I control — +1 strength; +1 stealth during diablerie actions; it cannot recruit
  allies or employ retainers.
- **Terrifying Visage** (1 pool): on a ready vampire who follows the Path of Cathari, one per vampire — if it is blocked, the blocking
  minion's controller burns 1 pool before block resolution. Every 'if blocked' effect is applied, ordered by the acting Methuselah,
  before any other card or effect [ANK 20220116] [RTR 19991206]; Change of Target does not stop it.
- **Monster** (archetype): on a ready vampire I control — once each turn (each player's turn [ANK 20180719-4]), after a combat
  involving it ends, it may burn 1 blood to unlock if the opposing minion is not ready; optional [ANK 20210309-3]; after combat,
  ordered with the other after-combat effects [ANK 20230314].
- **Saulot's Avenging Fist** (unique archetype, 2 pool, Salubri): on a Salubri I control — +1 strength; it can enter combat with a
  non-Salubri vampire as a (D) action, +1 stealth when that vampire is Tremere; the action requires a Salubri [LSJ 20080604].
- **Saulot's Guiding Wisdom** (unique archetype, title, 1 pool, Salubri): on an Independent Salubri I control — a unique Independent
  title worth 2 votes; the Salubri can lock during any referendum to force a vampire to abstain; once each turn it can lock before
  range is determined to end a combat involving another minion I control.
- **Regent** (title, 1 pool): on a Sabbat vampire I control with capacity 8 or more — the unique Sabbat title of regent (4 votes);
  Sabbat vampires can enter combat with it as a (D) action (an action requiring a Sabbat vampire [LSJ 20080604]); a contested regent
  is face down and its action unusable [LSJ 20070808-2].
- **Toreador Grand Ball** (1 pool, Toreador): choose two ready Toreador I control, put the card in play, lock one; the locked one
  does not unlock as normal; the other's non-bleed actions cannot be blocked, even after it attempts a bleed [LSJ 19970814]; the same
  vampire can be the unblockable one of several Grand Balls [TOM 19960528]. Minions can burn the card as a (D) action; Nosferatu get
  -1 stealth during that action.
- **Change of Target**: only if this minion is blocked, before block resolution — unlock it, the action ends unsuccessfully, it cannot
  perform the same action again this turn (the action reached resolution [LSJ 20070709]); played after the block, before the blocker
  locks and combat begins [LSJ 19980224]; the 'when blocked' effects still apply [RTR 19991206]; nothing is played after it
  [LSJ 20010803-1].
- **Scalpel Tongue** (1 blood, [cel] / [pre]; action modifier or reaction): only during the polling step of a political action —
  choose a vampire who has cast votes or ballots in this referendum; it is locked and abstains (its votes and ballots are cancelled);
  [CEL] / [PRE]: it also burns 1 blood. Not during a referendum that passes automatically [PIB 20150105].
- **Toreador Justicar** (political action, title): choose a ready Toreador (only a Camarilla vampire [LSJ 20041026]; not playable
  without a legal target or when the title is in play [LSJ 20100129]); a successful referendum puts the card on it as the unique
  Camarilla title of Toreador Justicar (3 votes); in this referendum each ready Toreador gets +1 vote.
