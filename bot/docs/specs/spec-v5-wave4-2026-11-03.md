# V5 wave 4 — the masters on a vampire, and Toreador's last cards (3 Nov 2026, bot v0.6.217)

Johan's decision (3 Nov 2026, RULINGS D-106, Claude's recommendation taken): wave 4 = the eight masters that go on a vampire
(Oblivion, Charisma, Depravity, Terrifying Visage, Monster, Saulot's Avenging Fist, Saulot's Guiding Wisdom, Regent) + Toreador Grand
Ball + Toreador's three last cards (Scalpel Tongue, Change of Target, Toreador Justicar), which share the masters' mechanisms (title
cards and their votes, the 'if blocked' step, the polling-step abstain). With them every Toreador row can turn playable — the first V5
precon that could join the menu (D-98). The eighteen masters 'in play' and Meditative Grove's cancel are wave 5. Sudden Reversal (Johan,
3 Nov, D-107): an 'as played' window, also against humans; its shape (a silent bot-to-bot grace + the human's own Hold on, or a
collective bot Hold on with 'No reaction' / the card) is wave 5's design.
This page is the wave's canon: the readings with their sources (§1; R-43), the protocol (§2), the decisions (§3; D-106: Claude's
design, not Johan's rulings), what v1 leaves out (§4; BOARD), the tests and the registered prediction (§5).

## 1. The readings (KRCG's v5 text; rulings by their vtes-rulings references)
- **One title per vampire** (the rulebook, quoted on the VEKN forum, 'A question about titles'): a vampire cannot have more than one
  title; a vampire that gains a title loses the one it had, and a lost title's card burns. A title card on a vampire therefore REPLACES
  its printed title. Votes by title (the rulebook's tables, cardfx's crypt `votes`): Justicar 3, regent 4, a '2 votes' title 2.
- **Oblivion** (Discipline master): on a vampire — +1 level of Oblivion (none → [obl], [obl] → [OBL]) and +1 capacity; never on a
  vampire with [OBL].
- **Charisma** (unique): on a ready vampire — its recruit-ally ACTIONS cost 1 blood or pool less (Piper's recruit is no action and
  gets no discount [LSJ 20090115-1]).
- **Depravity** (unique, 1 pool): on a ready vampire I control — +1 strength; +1 stealth during diablerie actions; it cannot recruit
  allies or employ retainers.
- **Terrifying Visage** (1 pool): on a ready vampire who follows the Path of Cathari, one per vampire — if it is blocked, the blocking
  minion's controller burns 1 pool before block resolution. Every 'if blocked' effect is applied, ordered by the acting Methuselah,
  before any other card or effect [ANK 20220116] [RTR 19991206]; Change of Target does not stop it.
- **Monster** (archetype): on a ready vampire I control — once each turn (each player's turn [ANK 20180719-4]), after a combat
  involving it ends, it may burn 1 blood to unlock if the opposing minion is not ready; optional [ANK 20210309-3]; after combat,
  ordered with the other after-combat effects [ANK 20230314].
- **Saulot's Avenging Fist** (unique archetype, 2 pool, Salubri): on a Salubri I control — +1 strength; it can enter combat with a
  non-Salubri vampire as a (D) action, +1 stealth when that vampire is Tremere; the action requires a Salubri [LSJ 20080604].
- **Saulot's Guiding Wisdom** (unique archetype, title, 1 pool, Salubri): on an Independent Salubri I control — a unique Independent
  title worth 2 votes; the Salubri can lock during any referendum to force a vampire to abstain; once each turn it can lock before
  range is determined to end a combat involving another minion I control.
- **Regent** (title, 1 pool): on a Sabbat vampire I control with capacity 8 or more — the unique Sabbat title of regent (4 votes);
  Sabbat vampires can enter combat with it as a (D) action (an action requiring a Sabbat vampire [LSJ 20080604]); a contested regent
  is face down and its action unusable [LSJ 20070808-2].
- **Toreador Grand Ball** (1 pool, Toreador): choose two ready Toreador I control, put the card in play, lock one; the locked one
  does not unlock as normal; the other's non-bleed actions cannot be blocked, even after it attempts a bleed [LSJ 19970814]; the same
  vampire can be the unblockable one of several Grand Balls [TOM 19960528]. Minions can burn the card as a (D) action; Nosferatu get
  -1 stealth during that action.
- **Change of Target**: only if this minion is blocked, before block resolution — unlock it, the action ends unsuccessfully, it cannot
  perform the same action again this turn (the action reached resolution [LSJ 20070709]); played after the block, before the blocker
  locks and combat begins [LSJ 19980224]; the 'when blocked' effects still apply [RTR 19991206]; nothing is played after it
  [LSJ 20010803-1].
- **Scalpel Tongue** (1 blood, [cel] / [pre]; action modifier or reaction): only during the polling step of a political action —
  choose a vampire who has cast votes or ballots in this referendum; it is locked and abstains (its votes and ballots are cancelled);
  [CEL] / [PRE]: it also burns 1 blood. Not during a referendum that passes automatically [PIB 20150105].
- **Toreador Justicar** (political action, title): choose a ready Toreador (only a Camarilla vampire [LSJ 20041026]; not playable
  without a legal target or when the title is in play [LSJ 20100129]); a successful referendum puts the card on it as the unique
  Camarilla title of Toreador Justicar (3 votes); in this referendum each ready Toreador gets +1 vote.

## 2. The protocol
- **Three new machine-read lines** (bot grammar; owed to main's §12 and the canary, the handoff):
  - COT: `VAMP plays Change of Target — unlocks; the action ends unsuccessfully.` The acting bot sends it after a successful block,
    before any combat line of its own; the bot that blocked THAT vampire (`_myBlockOf`) ends the combat it began at its verdict and
    unlocks its blocker (`_onCot` / `_cotHit`); a human's plain `VAMP plays Change of Target.` is read the same way. Event kind `cot`.
  - ABSTAIN: `VAMP plays Scalpel Tongue [LVL] — OWNER's TARGET is locked[, burns 1 blood] and abstains (N votes cancelled).` or
    `BEARER locks: Saulot's Guiding Wisdom — OWNER's TARGET abstains (N votes cancelled).` The caller has taken the votes off its
    count (`rc.against`, the voter's row, the shared counter); the voter's bot locks the vampire and burns its blood (`_onAbstain`).
    Event kind `abstain`.
  - burnAct: `VAMP takes an action to burn OWNER's CARD.` The owner's bot opens its block window (directed: only its minions block),
    and on its Pass burns the card (`OWNER burns CARD (VAMP's action).`); a human owner is told in chat. Event kind `burn-card`.
- **Widened:** L12.havenRush reads `(Saulot's Avenging Fist)` and `(Regent)` as well (m[4], the granting card; Haven's terms); a
  `Combat ends — X.` line wakes the answering bot's wait for the acting minion's first line (Saulot's Guiding Wisdom ends a combat
  before range); the block-bar line `Toreador Grand Ball — minions cannot block this action.` goes out before each non-bleed action of
  the Ball's free Toreador (every observing bot declines, keeps its reactions).
- **Table lines** (no reader; none ends with `plays X.`): the Ball's play line `SEAT plays Toreador Grand Ball: LOCKED is locked and
  does not unlock as normal; FREE's non-bleed actions cannot be blocked.`; `SEAT burns N pool (Terrifying Visage: VAMP was blocked).`;
  `VAMP burns 1 blood to unlock (Monster).`; `VAMP takes the title: Toreador Justicar (Justicar, 3 votes).`; `BEARER locks: Saulot's
  Guiding Wisdom — the combat of VAMP ends before range.`; the title referendum's terms `Referendum terms — Toreador Justicar: VAMP
  takes the title (Justicar, 3 votes).` (no pool term: every bot reads its stake as 0). Chat to a human: `Terrifying Visage: NAME burns
  N pool before the combat.`, `NAME: your Toreador Grand Ball is burned.`

## 3. The decisions (D-106: Claude's design under Johan's cut, not his rulings)
- **Hosts** (`_masterHostPlan`; a master that needs a host and has none is held before anything is paid): Oblivion on the ready vampire
  whose new level opens the most of my library's modes, then capacity, never on [OBL]; Charisma on a ready vampire that can recruit
  an ally I hold (held otherwise); Depravity on the biggest ready vampire that is not my only recruiter or employer; Terrifying Visage
  on the biggest ready Cathari without one; Monster and Saulot's Avenging Fist on the biggest archetype-free ready vampire (a Salubri for
  the Fist); the title cards where they gain the most votes (held when none gains; a Regent also while a regent is in play). A master
  that no vampire of mine (ready, in torpor, uncontrolled) may take is a dead card the discard phase cycles (`_masterNoHost`; the
  sim footprint: the lasombra smoke held Oblivion to the end while every vampire of its in play had [OBL]).
- **Toreador Grand Ball** (`_grandBallPlan`): the free Toreador can call a political card I hold (then the most votes, then blood); the
  locked one is the smallest unlocked other Toreador; held without a political card or a second Toreador; one Ball of mine at a time
  (a second is a dead card the discard phase cycles); the bar before every non-bleed announce of the free Toreador (17 sites: political,
  hunt, rescues, recruit, equip, employ, leave torpor, stock, strength, the card actions, the rushes, fetch, peek, pool damage, the
  burn action). A HUMAN's Ball names no pair: while one is in play no bot blocks a non-bleed action of that human's Toreador
  (`_humanBallFree`; a third Toreador of theirs goes unblocked, the price of not knowing).
- **The action that burns a Ball** (`_bestBurnCardFor`): only at my prey's or predator's card, scored (0.9 + 0.4 × its owner's votes / 4)
  × aggression × the plan's offence weight -- about a 1-pool bleed; a Nosferatu at -1 stealth.
- **Terrifying Visage:** the blocker burns at its own verdict (the card on the actor is public); its block scorer refuses a block whose
  pool would oust it, and a bleed no bigger than the pool the block costs.
- **Change of Target** (`_cotWorth`): only without a combat-ending card of mine for that vampire, when the blocker's expected strike
  puts it in torpor, or hurts (2+) against a bare fist. The vampire unlocks and the act-again machinery may revisit it, never for the
  same action (the turn ledger: a bleed, a political action and a card are booked by `_noteTurnAct`; `_noteCotAct` books a hunt, and a
  cardless action with its target [RTR 19950509]; a vampire whose mandatory hunt at 0 blood it ended is stuck [LSJ 19980112]). The
  blocker's bot writes no round row for the combat that never began.
- **Monster:** on my turn a board source of the act-again decision (1 blood); on another's turn when it keeps 1 blood (it can block again).
- **Saulot's Guiding Wisdom:** its 2 votes; the abstain on my own referendum (when it turns the count, once a referendum); the combat end
  for my blocked acting vampire's combat only, on Change of Target's test.
- **Scalpel Tongue:** on my own referendum (the action modifier), the superior mode when the vampire can, only when it turns the count,
  on 'a vampire who has cast votes': the bot's model is that a vampire's votes go together, so the target is one the seat's count
  names (the rest of its titles cannot make the count, and with it they do), the biggest; a count its titles cannot name (votes from
  cards) has no target. After the abstain the poll reopens: the table may answer it, as it may a boost.
- **Toreador Justicar:** the term is my ready Camarilla Toreador with the most to gain; its value is the votes gained (a pool-equivalent);
  the pass estimate counts the human seats' votes against (a bot has no pool at stake and holds its votes).
- **The granted rushes:** Haven Uncovered's machinery and doctrine (`_bestRushTargetFor`); the Fist at any non-Salubri vampire (+1 stealth
  at a Tremere), a Regent only by a Sabbat rusher (fx.rushHostBy). Every action through a card in play is booked once each turn per
  minion (rulebook 6.1.6, `_actSameKey`: the Haven or Regent by its host, the Fist on the actor, the Ball's burn by the card) --
  Haven Uncovered's own rush included, which had no such limit since v0.6.205.

## 4. What v1 leaves out
- **Reactions on another Methuselah's referendum:** Scalpel Tongue as a reaction and Saulot's Guiding Wisdom's abstain there.
- **Saulot's Guiding Wisdom's combat end** in a combat my vampire did not start as the acting minion (my blocker's, a rushed vampire's):
  the before-range step has no handshake between bots, so the answering side cannot end it before the acting side's first line.
- **The diablerie riders** (Depravity's stealth, Regent's move to the diablerist): bots never diablerize (O-15).
- **The texts' other targets:** Oblivion, Charisma, Terrifying Visage on another Methuselah's vampire; the table model does not read an
  opponent's Oblivion or Depravity (`_scanOpp` reads the printed disciplines).
- **The race at the before-range step:** a bot blocker may play Carrion Crows before the actor's Change of Target arrives.
- **A second Grand Ball** with another free Toreador; the other seats' ready Toreadors in Toreador Justicar's estimate (bots only).
- **A human blocker's Visage pool:** the bot tells the human; nothing checks it. **A human's Ball's pair** is not known (the bots spare
  all that human's Toreador on non-bleed actions). **Votes from cards** (ballots) hide a seat's voters from Scalpel Tongue.
- **Over-strict, legal:** `_bestStrengthFor` refuses a Preternatural Strength-class action on any vampire with a strength bonus
  (Depravity, the Fist: 'one per vampire' generalized to the key); `_inPlayNorms` counts my ash heap, so a burned unique master is
  never played again; `_uniqueInPlay` reads card names (a printed regent or justicar on a human's table is not 'the title in play').
- **Older, met on the way:** Saulot's Healing Touch is certified FULL by the auto tier, but its rescue discount and its blood-adding
  action are not built; compiler v1.9.8 only makes it an archetype (it now goes on a vampire, one archetype each).
