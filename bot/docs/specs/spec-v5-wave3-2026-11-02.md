# V5 wave 3 — the masters still played blind (2 Nov 2026, bot v0.6.215)

Johan's decision (2 Nov 2026, RULINGS D-104, Claude's recommendation taken): no rule for uncurated masters — a deck joins the menu
only when it is 100 % playable, so the released decks never meet one, and a human who gives a bot another deck sits at the table and
corrects it (the 14 July rule stands for that case). Wave 3 curates the masters by class instead: (a) the locations that LOCK for
stealth or intercept, (b) blood, (c) the out-of-turn masters, (e) the bleed riders; the masters that go on a vampire (d) and the rest
(f) are wave 4 (BOARD). Riding the round (D-92): two rules errors in the master finder, and main's KRCG v5 reply's name item.
This page is the wave's canon: the readings with their sources (§1; R-42), the protocol (§2), the decisions (§3; D-104: Claude's
design, not Johan's rulings), what v1 leaves out (§4; BOARD), the tests and the registered prediction (§5).

## 1. The readings

  a master card can play it'): the clan icon is one (VEKN forum, Ventrue Headquarters with the only Ventrue in torpor: not playable).
  Compiler v1.9.1's 'a clan icon on a master is not a requirement' (Hunting Grounds, Warsaw Station) was wrong; v1.9.6 lets curated
  masters inherit the data's clan. A hunting ground's icon gates the PLAY; who gains the blood is the text's ('a ready vampire you
  control'), now the mode's own fact (`bloodAddClan` / `bloodAddSect` / `bloodAddPath`).
- **Out-of-turn masters** are played only on another Methuselah's turn, at their own trigger, and use the next master phase action
  (the rulebook). 36 entries carry `oot` (a keyword segment: 'Out-of-turn.' / 'Master: out-of-turn.'); a master that only mentions
  out-of-turn cards does not (Bleeding the Vine, Vox Senis).
- **The locations** (each read from its text; costs from the data): Backways (Gangrel), Elysian Fields (Lasombra), Fortune Teller
  Shop (Ravnos), Opium Den (Follower of Set = Ministry): lock for +1 stealth to that clan; Anarch Railroad: to an Anarch; Creepshow
  Casino: 'as a vampire you control announces an undirected action … even if stealth is not yet needed'. Market Square: +1
  intercept to an Assamite (Banu Haqim); Channel 10: +2, 'not usable during the first action in a minion phase' [LSJ 20100505, ANK
  20210124]; KRCG News Radio: +1 to a minion I control; Wall Street Night: +1 'during an undirected action'.
- **Blood:** Carfax Abbey (requires a ready Anarch; a ready Anarch gains 1, a second with a ready baron); Burial Site Hunting Ground
  (a follower of the Path of Death and the Soul; the crypt's `path`, compiler v1.9.6); Art Museum (lock in the influence phase: a
  Toreador in uncontrolled), Ecoterrorists (a Gangrel), Powerbase: Montreal (any, no lock); Unholy Sacrament (3 to a titled Sabbat
  vampire in uncontrolled); Family Gathering (trifle: the top crypt card if a Hecata, with 1 blood; not with an empty crypt [RTR
  20000501]).
- **Bleed riders:** Haqim's Law: Retribution — each copy once per action [ANK 20221102-2], by the acting bleeding minion [ANK
  20220502], before resolution [ANK 20231124]; Club Illusion — 1 blood at the announce, once; it does not make the action one

- **Archon Investigation:** a bleed of 4 or more at me, after blocks are declined: burn the acting minion, the action is not
  successful; before anything played 'as the action would be successful' [LSJ 19980105]; it targets the bleeding vampire [LSJ

## 2. The protocol
- **One new machine-read line** (ARCHON_BURN): `SEAT plays Archon Investigation — VAMP is burned (the bleed of N is not successful).`
  The target bot sends it (after its pay line), clears its pending bleed and says `Pass`; the acting bot reads it in `_onLog`, flags
  the action, and when its window answers it burns the minion (and the cards on it) with no pool moved and no Edge. Against a human
  actor the bot asks in chat. Owed to main's §12 and the canary (the handoff).
- **Table lines** (no reader): `VAMP locks CARD: +N stealth.` (The Labyrinth's shape), `VAMP locks CARD: +N intercept.`, `VAMP
  discards CARD (Haqim's Law: Retribution): +1 bleed.` followed by the frozen L12.add line, `VAMP burns 1 blood (Club Illusion): +1
  bleed.` before the announce line, `SEAT uses|locks CARD to add 1 blood to an uncontrolled vampire.`, `SEAT adds N blood to an
  uncontrolled vampire (Unholy Sacrament).`, `SEAT reveals a Hecata from the top of the crypt and draws it (Family Gathering).`
  None ends with `plays X.` (L12.actObs); Creepshow's lock line goes out BEFORE the announce line (the window rule).

## 3. The decisions
- **The master finder** (`_bestMasterFor`): never an `oot` card; `_masterReqOk` (a ready vampire meeting clan / sect / any printed
  title — city titles are not told apart); `_masterHold` for one-shots without a target: Unholy Sacrament needs a titled Sabbat in
  uncontrolled with room for all 3 and the 3 pool above the influence floor (`_poolFloorEff`); Family Gathering needs a crypt list
  that is all Hecata (the bot cannot see its top card).
- **Lock sources** are spent like The Labyrinth: only when a block needs answering (R-12), first on a tie with a hand card, once per
  action; the stacks count every source. Without the action at hand (block odds, `interceptPotential` estimates without one) the
  conditional intercepts (Channel 10, Wall Street Night) fail closed. Channel 10 reads `_turnActN` (the action rows of this turn).
- **Creepshow Casino** at a recruit, an employ, an equip or a political announce of mine, never a hunt; once a turn (it locks).
- **Haqim's Law:** in my own modifier window after blocks are declined, before the hand modifiers; a dead combat card (D-59) always;
  otherwise only while two combat cards stay in hand, or when the point ousts the prey; the card I hold most copies of.
- **Club Illusion:** when the Anarch keeps 2 blood after paying, or the point ousts the prey.
- **Archon Investigation:** at my own decline of the block (only my minions may block a bleed at me) when the bleed is 4+, before a
  bounce or a block; or when a modifier reopens the window at 4+. Pool after paying ≥ 1 (R-34); `_ootDebt` takes the next master
  phase's action.
- **Names:** an article-first alias for every v3 key ending ', The' / ', An' / ', A' (main's reply 5.2): the client's v5 names now
  resolve to the same card for all 4,149 v3 cards (measured; `An Anarch Manifesto` was the one miss).
