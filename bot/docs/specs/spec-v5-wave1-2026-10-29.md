# V5 wave 1 — additional strikes, aggravated strikes, Villein (29 Oct 2026, bot v0.6.213)

Johan's decision (RULINGS D-98, 29 Oct): the V5 waves go by EFFECT CLASS as `CARDS.md`'s class map ranks them; wave 1 = Villein,
the extra strikes (Quickness, Pursuit, Arms of Ahriman) and the aggravated strikes (Claws of the Dead, Sculpt the Flesh, Wall of
Filth and the rest of the `aggr` row). This page is the wave's canon: the readings with their sources (§1; RULINGS R-39), the
protocol on the wire (§2) and the decisions (§3) (D-99: Claude's design and thresholds, not Johan's rulings), what v1 leaves
out (§4; the BOARD's riders) and the tests (§5).

## 1. The readings
- **Additional strikes** (the rulebook's "Additional strikes"; ANK on the VEKN forum, threads 79477 / 79975). They come after the
  first pair is completed (both strikes resolved, the damage taken). The acting minion decides first whether to gain one, then the
  opposing minion, and the impulse returns (a minion that passed may still answer the other's). Each is another choose-strike /
  resolve-strike step at the same range: in pairs while both have one, alone after that. One LIMITED source a round per minion;
  Quickness [CEL] is outside the limit [LSJ 20001206], and a vampire plays one Quickness a round. A combatant no longer ready ends
  the combat. Quickness [cel] and Pursuit [CEL] are limited; Arms of Ahriman [OBL] is a dodge that brings one limited strike, and
  that strike is not optional: once a limited additional strike was used this round, [OBL] cannot be played for the dodge alone
  (Dust Up's wording [ANK 20220204]).
- **Aggravated damage.** The bot prevents aggravated damage first (the defender's choice, never worse; D-22). Kevlar Vest ("1
  damage from any other source") and Guardian Angel ("1 damage each combat") prevent aggravated damage too. A vampire cannot mend aggravated damage: its first
  unprevented point torpors a ready vampire.
- **Claws of the Dead** [pro] (1 blood): the round's hand strikes are aggravated, additional ones included; playable as late as
  after both strikes are chosen, before they resolve [RTR 19960112] (the bot plays it with its own strike). **Sculpt the Flesh** [pro]: an aggravated hand strike; requires a
  Tzimisce. **Sword of the Archangel**: strength+1 aggravated, Salubri only. **Ivory Bow**: 1R aggravated.
- **Invigorate** (requires an Anarch) is an action modifier: played before the action resolves; once the action is blocked the
  combat is its resolution, so [pro] (hand strikes aggravated) and [ani] (+1 strength) last the whole action.
- **Taste of Vitae** counts the blood lost to every pair of the round.
- **Villein** (trifle): 2–5 blood from a vampire I control to my pool; +1 pool for each Villein already on that vampire: the cost
  must be payable from the pool when the card is played (no third Villein at 1 pool) [ANK 20210714] and is paid as the pool comes
  [LSJ 20020620]; less blood at resolution = what is there [LSJ 20100216].
- **Wall of Filth** is a PREVENTION card, not an aggravated strike: played before range, burned to prevent 2 non-aggravated
  damage ([PRO] / [THA]: any 2); fewer points coming = all of them, the rest is lost [RTR 20041202] [ANK 20200318].
- **Rutor's Hand** (+1 stealth action, 1 blood): its aggravated damage is its own price ([THA]: burn 3 blood to be immune; at
  [tha] it torpors a ready vampire, and the unlock pays only after the vampire leaves torpor); its unlock is locking the card,
  once a minion phase.

## 2. The protocol
- **My declaration** is main's `NAME gets an additional strike.` (`CMB_LINE.extra`) + the tally's `xa` / `xb`, one line per
  strike gained. An aggravated strike is `NAME strikes for N aggravated damage.` (`CMB_LINE.agg`) with `ga` / `gb` = N and `a` /
  `b` = 0; with an additional pair the bot writes `sp: 2` and its side's ROUND totals (`a` / `b`, `ga` / `gb`; `pa` / `pb` = the
  round's prevention).
- **Between bots, a round where nobody holds a source sends no new line and makes no new wait.** The ACTING bot decides first and
  declares BEFORE its round line, so the answering bot (which resolved its first pair earlier) reads the declaration from that
  line on. The answering bot waits for that round line only when it holds a source; otherwise it meets the declaration in its
  press-step wait, which the declaration ends (the strikes come before the press). After the acting bot declared, the answering
  bot answers with its own declaration or the new bot line `NAME combat — VAMPIRE does not take an additional strike.` (the
  fourth "nothing" line; handoff to main of 29 Oct). A pair is struck acting first; the side that took a strike says its round line
  and the striker waits for it. The acting bot checks for a late declaration again before its press decision, and its press wait
  returns at once when one came before the wait was registered (the egress review's race: a synchronous relay, a watched duel).
- **Against a human** the bot asks only when somebody declared ("Any additional strikes?" after its own declaration, "What is your
  additional strike?" for theirs), inside the Strike step. When the human is the ACTING minion and the bot would take one, the
  bot asks first and declares after (R-39), and asks once more after its declaration when the human passed (the impulse
  returns). A human's declaration in the Damage step (the Combat menu's *Additional strike* row) reopens the strikes before the
  bot steps past Damage, while the bot's vampire is still in the combat; the client's taken line then counts every pair.
- **Byte for byte.** The sim and hotseat bridges deliver lines synchronously, so every new `await` sits behind a synchronous
  check: a round without a source is the old round, trace for trace (the footprint shows only matches where a source was held).

## 3. The decisions
- An aggravated strike outranks any plain one against a vampire (`_bestStrikeFor`: +10), never against an ally; my aggravated hit
  is a finisher in the dodge rule (D-30) against a vampire only. An additional strike is taken when it deals damage to a minion still in the combat
  (`decide('add-strike')`).
- Claws of the Dead [pro] when it makes the round's hand strike an aggravated hit that lands and leaves the vampire 1 blood (0 only
  when nothing comes at it). Wall of Filth goes on in the first before-range step it can, and burns at its full 2 or when the
  strike would wound.
- Villein: the Blood Doll drain's rule for N (D-45) — every blood on the edge of an oust; otherwise the vampire keeps its reserve,
  1 blood and its place over the hunt bar when it has an action; held when nobody can spare 2.
- Rutor's Hand only at [THA] with 1 + 3 + 1 blood (cost, immunity, one left), scored 1.5 × the plan's `econW` (the tier-1
  equipment's weight); then a free act-again source.
- Invigorate on a rush only, [pro] before [ani]; [dom] has no consumer yet.

## 4. What v1 leaves out
- **Additional strikes:** one source a round per side (so Quickness [CEL]'s outside-the-limit rider never stacks yet); no impulse
  back to the acting bot after it passed (a declaration that reaches the acting bot after its press decision is resolved before
  it reads the other side's press — its own press line came first); no dodge, steal or combat ends in an additional pair; no strike card that brings one (Backstab-class: a strike and
  `addStrike` in one mode); a human's declaration after the bot stepped past Damage; Taste of Vitae is decided before the presses.
- **Requirements:** `req.clan` / `req.sect` (`_entryReqOk`) are read by the strike, dodge, additional-strike, aggravated-hands and
  equipment finders and for Invigorate; bleed cards, modifiers, reactions, allies, politics and the prevent, press, maneuver and
  combat-ends finders (Touch of Valeren) still read the discipline only (the multi-clan Path precons will meet it); a card's Path icon (`path` in vtes.json: Wall of Filth carries Path of Caine) is not read
  as a requirement yet (the pathcaine crypt all follows Caine).
- **Amber riders:** Sculpt the Flesh [PRO] (the card on the opposing minion, its unlock-phase burn, the +1 stealth action to burn
  it); the Sword's cancel (a grapple or aim card for 1 blood) and its unlock; Kevlar Vest's 2 against gun strikes
  (`_equipPrevent` prevents 1); Invigorate [dom] (allies cannot block) and before a block on actions other than a rush; the
  strength+N weapon family beyond the Sword (compile as `strBonus`).
- **Latent, unreachable with today's decks** (the grading, 29 Oct): Arms of Ahriman [OBL] would be offered as a plain dodge after
  the limit was used (the dodge is offered at the first strike only); Claws of the Dead checks its own blood, not a strike card's
  picked the same round (the Gangrel precon has no strike cards). Older, shared: no prevention (the
  Wall included) is offered against Carrion Crows' damage; the maneuver finders only maneuver to long; Claws is played at the
  first strike only.
- **First-round-restricted strikes** (compiler v1.9.4: 'Not usable during / on the first round', 9 cards, Walk of Flame in
  banuhaqim) fail closed: never played, as the four tagged before. Played from round 2 once the finders read the combat round.
