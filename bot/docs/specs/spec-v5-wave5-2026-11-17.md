## 1. The readings (KRCG's v5 text; rulings by their references; graded in the round by the charter)
- **Tension in the Ranks** (unique): after a READY minion (vampire or ally) is burned or sent to torpor, its controller burns 1
  pool; not for an imbued [LSJ 20060409-2]; simultaneous losses ordered by the acting Methuselah [LSJ 20090208-2]. Any Methuselah may
  burn it with a master phase action and two master cards discarded.
- **Anarch Revolt** (not unique): a Methuselah who controls no ready Anarch by the END of their unlock phase burns 1 pool
  [LSJ 20080106], per copy in play [LSJ 19980225]. A vampire can call a referendum to burn it as a +1 stealth political action
  (R-14: the printed stealth is the total).
- **Frontal Assault** (not unique): each ready minion I control can enter combat with a minion of my prey as a (D) action (0
  stealth, once per minion: rulebook 6.1.6); +1 pool after a ready minion of my prey is burned or sent to torpor (any cause); at my
  influence phase the card burns and I burn 1 pool per ready minion of my prey; copies stack [LSJ 20061018].
- **Forward Momentum** (unique): after my Cathari bleeds, +1 counter if it landed (1 or more), else −1; after my Cathari performs an
  action, 2 counters may unlock it.
- **Privileged Position** (unique; to PLAY: 2+ ready vampires on the Path of Power and the Inner Voice, R-42): after a referendum
  called by my vampire passes, lock it: my prey burns 1 pool.
- **Mob Connections** (unique): lock: a minion of mine gets 1 press, only to continue combat; minions can burn it as a (D) action.
- **Garibaldi-Meucci Museum** (unique location): (a) my unlock phase, lock + 1 pool: a hand card for a card in my ash heap whose
  requirements name Anarch or baron [ANK 20250320, LSJ 20080603, LSJ 20050128], both named at once [LSJ 20000222]; (b) before range:
  lock to end a combat between my Anarch and another Anarch (end-of-round / end-of-combat cards still play [RTR 20001020]).
- **Powerbase: Los Angeles** (unique location): my discard phase, lock: +1 discard phase action; if it discards a card requiring an
  Anarch or making a vampire Anarch (a baron card counts [LSJ 20050128]), unlock a ready Anarch. Other Methuselahs' Anarchs can steal
  it as a (D) action (an action requiring an Anarch [LSJ 20080604]).
- **Carver's Meat Packing and Storage** (unique location, 1 pool): a vampire of capacity 3 or less that goes to torpor gets a hostage
  counter; a hostage cannot be moved to ready or be diablerized; my master phase, lock: X blood to a ready vampire of mine (X = the
  vampires with hostage counters); any unlock phase, any ready vampire may burn 2 blood to burn any vampire's hostage counters (once a
  turn [ANK 20200729]); the counters burn when the card leaves play.
- **The Rack** (unique location): as it is played or changes controller, the controller chooses a ready vampire; at the
  controller's unlock phase that vampire can gain 2 blood (in torpor too [PIB 20150522]; it follows the vampire out and back
  [LSJ 20010809-2]; no new choice for a vampire that falls out of contention [RTR 19960112]). Other Methuselahs' vampires can
  steal it as a (D) action; the new controller chooses [ANK 20200130-2].
- **The Hungry Coyote** (unique location, 2 pool): my Sabbat vampires get +1 hunt, special hunts included [RTR 20030519].
- **The Coven** (unique trifle): lock: 2 blood to a ready vampire of mine (not mid-healing [PIB 20121031]); at my discard phase my
  predator takes control (mandatory [LSJ 20100723, PIB 20111002]); the trifle's action comes even if it contests.
- **Piper** (requires a ready Anarch): lock a ready unlocked Anarch: it recruits or employs an ally or retainer from my hand
  (requirements and cost as normal); not an action, cannot be blocked, no Charisma [LSJ 20090115-1]; the ally cannot act this turn
  [RBK recruit-ally].
- **Día de los Muertos** (one per game, 1 pool): the first referendum a Sabbat vampire of mine calls this turn passes automatically:
  no polling, no "during a political action / referendum" effects, margin effects do nothing [LSJ 19980107]; no Scalpel Tongue
  [PIB 20150105]; no Yewon Ong / Alexa Draper discard (D-111 (3)).
- **Cursed Abattoir** (unique location): after a zombie ally or retainer of mine is burned, +1 counter; during a bleed, burn 1: a
  zombie ally of mine gets +1 bleed.
- **Week of Nightmares** (one per game, 10 counters; 5b): every Ravnos (any controller) +1 bleed, +1 strength, no normal hunt; a
  Ravnos can steal 1 blood from another Ravnos as a +1 stealth hunt action (directed at a vampire, it can fail on an empty one, hunt
  bonuses apply [LSJ 19970224, LSJ 20050720-2, RTR 20180511]); at each Methuselah's unlock phase they may move 1 counter to a Ravnos;
  at no counters every Ravnos burns 1 blood per counter on it or is burned, then the card burns.
- **Yawp Court** (unique location, a ready Sabbat to play; 5b): when a political action is successful, before the referendum: lock it
  and a ready unlocked Sabbat of mine to enter combat with the acting vampire; still ready after → my Sabbat takes 2 environmental
  damage and the referendum goes on; not ready → no referendum [LSJ 20060902]; the action's cost is paid first [LSJ 20080201].
- **Meditative Grove** (5b): lock to cancel a frenzy card as it is played ON a Salubri I control (its cost still paid). The frenzy is
  the opponent's when a card hits the opposing minion (Dam the Heart's River [ANK 20220409]); Rage of Apedemak, the only frenzy card in
  our decks (pathcaine), is played on its own vampire — between bots the cancel never fires.
- **Sudden Reversal** (out-of-turn; 5b): cancel a master as another Methuselah plays it, its cost not paid; only masters played from
  the hand [RTR 20001020]; a cancelled trifle gives no extra action [LSJ 20090626]; it uses my next master phase action (D-104).

## 2. The engine: a card's worth over its life (O-41 generalised)
Today a card in play has three prices: the Ball's burn (`(0.9 + 0.4 × votes/4) × aggression × offW`), Smiling Jack's (D-114 /
D-115: a seat's price over H = min(its runway, Jack's life)) and T6's generic 0.3–0.6 at the window of an action at my card; the
master finder plays any master (Johan's 14 July rule). O-41 is where that breaks: my Jack's worth sets my prey's loss over her
runway against my payments over Jack's "whole life" — my own runway, 20–40 rounds at a quiet predator — while the game itself ends
when the others are gone (5–8 rounds at those tables). The proposal is one valuation for every card in play:

- **worth_V(C) = Σ over seats s of w_V(s) × Σ over the rounds t < H_s of f_s(t)**, in pool units (the scorer's currency).
- **f_s(t)** is the card's flow to seat s in round t: pool; blood (1 blood on a vampire with room = 1 pool, home pricing's rate); an
  action (that vampire's best action, the scorer's value); a bleed point (1 pool × P(the bleed lands)); a press; a hunt. Each card
  has a small worth handler (`CARD_WORTH`, CARD_HANDLERS' pattern: instruction, not vocabulary) that reads its rates from the table
  model — the event log's rows per seat a round, blended with a prior per card that the measurement sets from the V5 smoke.
- **The horizon (O-41's first half):** H_s = min(R_s, L). R_s is the seat's own runway with the card's drain counted (D-115's root
  where the drain grows). L, the card's life, is min(its owner's runway, **the expected end of the game**) — a new plan read,
  `plan.reads.gameEnd`: the longest runway among the other live seats, every known drain counted. Every flow of the card, its owner's
  payments included, stops where the game would stop.
- **The seats (O-41's second half):** one shared seat valuation, `_seatW(s)`: me `wDef` (1; 2 when my runway is shorter than my turns
  to oust my prey, `spec-jack-burn-2026-11-13.md` §3), my prey `offW`, every other seat 0.15 (the referendum's "everyone else a little", `_refValue`). One
  function, so the meta layer's §8 law can set it per seat later; `_refValue` itself is not touched here.
- **The four consumers.** (1) *Play:* the master finder ranks a card that has a worth handler by its worth less its cost; worth ≤ 0
  is held, and the discard phase cycles a card that stays dead; the 14 July rule stays for cards without a handler (no menu deck has
  one after the wave). (2) *Defend:* the window of an action that burns or steals my card weighs the card's worth to me (T6 stays
  for cards without a handler). (3) *Attack:* an action at another seat's card is worth P(success) × (w(owner) × its worth to its
  owner, plus my own worth as the new controller for a steal), against my best other action as today; Anarch Revolt's burn
  referendum and Tension's master-phase burn are priced the same way. (4) *Cancel (5b):* Sudden Reversal cancels a master whose
  weighed worth to its owner beats my next master phase action.
- **Jack and the Ball run through it behind a knob, `cardWorth`.** 0 = v0.6.229 byte for byte (D-114 / D-115 and the Ball's
  formula; the master finder's order); 1 = the engine for them too. The new cards' decisions read the engine regardless: they are new
  decisions with no previous behaviour but "tabled blind" (wave 4's precedent); their rules (payments, triggers, control) are rules
  and never behind a knob (knob hygiene).
- **O-41's rows** (computed with the bot's own `_jackPriceAt`, Jack with no counter or 2, my predator quiet):

| row (suite) | today (D-115) | my payments over her horizon | the game's end, my prey only | the game's end + every seat (proposed) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| my 40 against her 30 (171d) | 0 | 21.74 | 21.74 | 24.74 |
| my 12 against her 30 (171d) | 18 | 21.74 | 21.74 | 24.74 |
| my 20 against her 20, 2 counters (169d) | 0 | 11.40 | 10.70 | 13.70 |
| my 12 against her 20, 2 counters (169d) | 3.70 | 11.40 | 10.70 | 13.70 |

  (the flat runway of v0.6.225 read 12.22 for 169d's 20 against 20). The game ends at 8.26 rounds in 171d (her runway: the other
  seat's is 6.84) and at 5.00 in 169d, where today's rule bills me for 40 and 20.
- **Left out of v1:** the removal hazard. A burnable card lives short at bot tables (Jack's median life 1–2 rounds, v0.6.226; a
  Ball 0.52 rounds, the bench round), and v1's L ignores it; the measurement reports each burnable card's life, and the hazard enters
  only if the defend or attack reads misbehave.

## 3. 5a — the cards: mechanics, lines, decisions
Every pool loss below has R-34's oust check beside it; every new machine-read line gets its parser, its `_evClassify` kind, a
section through `_onLog` and a §12 entry in the handoff; a human who must act by hand is told in chat by the card's owner bot (the
human-backup constant).
- **Tension in the Ranks.** Each bot pays for its own minions as they fall (`SEAT burns 1 pool (Tension in the Ranks: VAMP went to
  torpor).`, a table line); a human's minion: the owner bot reminds that human. Worth: Σ w(s) × (−1) × the seat's minion losses a
  round. Banu Haqim (snipe) and Path of Caine (chase) put the losses on their prey, so they play it. The burn: in my master phase,
  when the card costs me more than my two weakest masters and the action.
- **Anarch Revolt.** Paid at the end of each bot's own unlock phase in `_menaceUnlockEngine` (`SEAT burns 1 pool (Anarch Revolt: no
  ready Anarch).`); a human's unlock: the owner bot reminds. The burn: a political action with no card, terms
  `Referendum terms — Anarch Revolt: burn NAME's Anarch Revolt.` (a new burn term: every bot's stake is what the card takes from it
  over its H; the voters vote their stakes as today), stealth 1; on a pass the owner's bot burns it. At the V5 smoke every starter
  pays 1 a round (Tzimisce and the other Anarch decks pay nothing), so the political starters should call the burn.
- **Frontal Assault.** Played by the look-ahead rule (D-58): each minion's scorer is asked with the granted rush in its ctx; played
  only when the rushes it would take beat the bill (1 pool per prey's ready minion still standing at my influence phase, less the
  expected torpors), so mostly a finisher against a prey with one or two ready minions. The rushes are `_bestGrantRushFor`'s (the
  Fist's and the Regent's shape: 0 stealth, booked once per minion and card); +1 pool on each loss of my prey's (her board and the
  taken lines); the bill at the start of my influence phase (`_influenceLoop`), before any transfer, with its line.
- **Forward Momentum.** Counters on the card (`counters`, the memo's token); +1 / −1 after each Cathari bleed of mine; after an
  action, 2 counters unlock the vampire when the act-again decision would act again (Monster's slot as a board source).
- **Privileged Position.** After my passed referendum: `SEAT locks Privileged Position — NAME burns 1 pool.` (new, machine-read: the
  prey's bot pays with its oust check; a human prey is told). Always, while the prey is live (the lock-income precedent).
- **Mob Connections.** `_wantPress` counts it as a press to continue (never to end); the press goes out in the handshake's frozen
  shape, `VAMP presses to continue (Mob Connections).`; the burn and its defence through the engine.
- **Garibaldi-Meucci Museum.** (a) At my unlock: when the hand holds a dead card (`_deadHandIndex`) and my ash heap an Anarch card
  my hand would play, pool above the floor: `SEAT locks Garibaldi-Meucci Museum and burns 1 pool: CARD for CARD.` (b) Before range,
  my acting Anarch against an Anarch, on Change of Target's test: Saulot's Guiding Wisdom's pair of lines with this card's name
  (wave 4's before-range limit stands: my blocker's combats are not reached).
- **Powerbase: Los Angeles.** At my discard phase: lock when I hold a dead card; an Anarch card discarded unlocks my most useful
  locked Anarch. **The steal** (new, the burn action's twin): `VAMP takes an action to steal NAME's CARD.` → the owner's bot opens
  its block window (directed); on its Pass it gives the card (`give`, its id kept) and writes `NAME gives CARD to SEAT (VAMP’s
  action).`; the stealer's bot takes it in `_onGiven` and makes the card's choices. A human owner is told to hand it over.
- **Carver's Meat Packing and Storage.** Each bot applies the hostage rule to its own small vampires (a memo token on the vampire;
  the rescue and leave-torpor finders refuse a hostage); the owner counts X from the public boards; lock in my master phase when X ≥ 1
  and a vampire has room; my own hostage is freed at an unlock when it is worth a rescue and the payer keeps 1 blood.
- **The Rack.** The choice is a memo token (`The Rack: VAMP`): the vampire with room that acts most; +2 blood at my unlock
  (`_applyPhaseIncome`'s shape); the steal as Powerbase's.
- **The Hungry Coyote.** The hunt's gain reads it (+1 for my Sabbat, a hunt card's own gain included).
- **The Coven.** Lock before my discard phase when a ready vampire has room (+2 blood); at my discard phase `give` to my predator,
  locked as it is; the receiver's unlock unlocks it. Worth: +2 now and each lap, less the laps it gives the table, my predator first.
- **Piper.** By the look-ahead rule: when the minion phase's scorer would take a recruit or an employ for that Anarch this turn and a
  seat could block it, Piper does it in the master phase, unblockable (Tzimisce: Revenant, Underbridge Stray).
- **Día de los Muertos.** By the look-ahead rule: when a ready Sabbat of mine would call a referendum this turn whose
  (1 − P(pass)) × value is worth the card; the caller writes `Referendum passes automatically — CARD (Día de los Muertos).` in place
  of the poll (new, machine-read: no bot polls, boosts or abstains).
- **Cursed Abattoir.** Built (a zombie is KRCG's "Zombie" in the ally's text); its counters and the bleed rider wait for a zombie the
  bot plays — Path of Death's four zombie allies are inert (Gravebound Drone 8, Freakish Conglomeration 3, Fiorella 1, Heartrender 1),
  so its row reads 'unseen' until their wave.

## 4. D-107 — the 'as played' window (5b; Johan's choice of shape)
Common to every shape: a master a bot plays waits for its window before anything is paid or done; cancelled, it goes to the ash heap,
nothing paid, no effect, no trifle action, the master phase action spent. A bot holding Sudden Reversal reads another seat's master
play (its fx clone or the client's play line) and answers at once, `SEAT plays Sudden Reversal — cancels NAME's CARD (its cost is
not paid).` (new, machine-read), books `_ootDebt`, and the first answer wins. The decision is the engine's (§2 (4)). Measured on
v0.6.229: about 30 master picks a match at a five-bot table (12 matches).
- **A — silent (Claude's proposal of 3 Nov).** Between bots: a short grace after the play (~0.5 s on the real clock; it runs inside
  the beat a bot waits anyway before its next act, so the client's pace sees nothing new; free in the sim). A bot's master with a
  human seated: the bot's beat is the window (~3 s at Standard); a human says Hold on (`_holdGate` stops it) and plays the card. A
  human's master: a bot answers at once and tells the human in chat to take it back. No new bubbles; a cancel after the window is
  too late (the bot says so).
- **B — collective (Johan's idea of 3 Nov).** After every master, one bot speaks for the bots: `Hold on…` (its thinking announce —
  §12.1: a bot's Hold on holds nothing), then `No reaction` or the card. Visible; about 60 bubbles a five-player match, and at a
  table with a human the window must be human-length (the reaction clock: ~30 × 3 s a match at Standard).
- **C — ask the humans.** A between bots; at a table with a human each bot master is followed by `Any reaction?` (say 9) and the
  tempo's react clock (Adaptive skips a human with an empty hand); a human's `No reaction` ends it. About 24 questions a match with
  one human and four bots.
- **Recommendation: A.** It is how a physical table works (whoever holds the cancel speaks as the card lands), it costs no tempo and
  no bubbles, and its human half is the Hold on the table already uses. B or C if the window should be seen.
- **Meditative Grove's cancel** uses the same notion inside combat: a human's frenzy card played against my Salubri → `OWNER locks
  Meditative Grove — CARD is cancelled (its cost is still paid).` and the human takes the effect back. Between bots it never fires.
