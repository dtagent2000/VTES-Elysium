## 1. The dodge (decided with Johan — points 1–3)

**Today** (`decide('combat-strike')`, 5086): a KNOWN strike (it came first) is dodged only when it would empty or
torpor me or is aggravated; an UNKNOWN strike only at blood ≤ 1.

**The rule** (Johan, 17:47 + 18:06):
1. Known strike: dodge when what it does to me (damage + steal; crows excluded — they land anyway) is MORE than what
   my own strike would deal this round. Aggravated: always. The survival rule stays (it would empty or torpor me).
2. Two or more usable dodges in hand: the tie goes to the dodge.
3. Unknown strike (I strike first): dodge at an expected strike ≥ 2 that is also more than mine (the tie rule as in
   2), using the expected strike Majesty already reads (`_ceCtx` → `_expStrikeDmg`, 1 + 2 × `oppCombat`; 0 at long
   range against a seat that has shown no ranged strike). With `combatEndStats` 0 the unknown case keeps today's
   blood ≤ 1.
4. Later (knowledge layer, not R4): a deck that never hits for more than 1 → dodge against 1 too.

**My output this round** = the strike the bot would actually make, computed by ONE pure helper cut out of the
strike choice (6227–6283): `_strikePick(vamp)` → `{ kind: steal | strike | fist | none, card, out }` with the same
finders and arithmetic — `_bestStrikeFor`, `_bestStealStrikeFor` with `_stealAvail()`, the fist `1 + strength + crypt
mod` (0 at long range), `_noStrikeRound`, the `stealOk` rule under `combatMind`. The strike site then plays
`_strikePick`'s answer, so the dodge compares against exactly what would have been struck (no hand splice in the
helper). My own crows are not part of `out` (they land whether I dodge or not).

**The floor** (open question A): "for now, against everything with 2 or more damage" — I read the floor of 2 as
holding for the tie rule too (1 vs 1 with two dodges: strike), so point 4 stays the only way to dodge a 1. At long
range with fists only (`out` 0) a ranged strike for 2 is dodged, a ranged 1 is not.

**Knob** `dodgeOut` (default 1; 0 = v0.6.204's rule byte for byte), in the manifest's knob list (2577), so the larger
re-measure after R4 can bench it. Mechanism count in the smoke: the `decide` rows' tags (`known-out`, `known-tie`,
`unknown-exp`, `survival`, …).

**Tests:** a new section (146): known 2 vs fist 1 → dodge; 2 vs Roundhouse 3 → strike; 2 vs strike card 2 → strike
with one dodge, dodge with two; 1 vs 1 with two → strike; long range: ranged 2 vs fist 0 → dodge; steal 2 → dodge;
aggravated 1 → dodge; survival (1 at blood 1) → dodge; unknown: expected 2.2 vs fist 1 → dodge, 1.8 → strike, knob
`combatEndStats` 0 → blood ≤ 1; `dodgeOut` 0 → the old answers; `_strikePick` against the strike the round really
plays (same card, same number) in a two-bot relay. 143d moves to the new rule (its 'take a 2 at blood 3' is now a
dodge against a fist for 1), a copy pinned under `dodgeOut: 0`.

next turn. **Tests:** the four arm shapes, a non-prey actor ignored, the pick by logTo and by chat, a pick from
another seat ignored, a card I do not hold.
