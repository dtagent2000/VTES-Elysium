## 1. A misread the cards bring up: "… during an action directed at you (or a card you control)"
- The sentence is a condition on 12 cards in the pool ("Only usable during …" on 8, "During an action directed at you …, X can …"
  on 4). The compiler reads it on none and no finder asks it, so the bot spends the card at any action. Five are in the decks:
  Eyes of Argus [aus] (malkavian 5, tremere 6, salubri 4, hecata 3, pathcathari 3), The Warrens (nosferatu 4; the header), Deep
  Ecology [ani] (gangrel 6), One With the Land (tzimisce 4; the header, both modes), Underbridge Stray (nosferatu 2, tzimisce 2;
  its burn to unlock a minion, not its recruit or its press).
- Measured on v0.6.232's 240 (the four starters; an instrumented copy, the same seeds): Eyes of Argus's +2 intercept was played
  555 times, **371 at an undirected action** (a political action 131, a stock 69, an equip 68, a tabling 53, a hunt 48, strength
  2) and 184 at a bleed or a rush; The Warrens 274 times, **197 undirected** (stock 69, hunt 66, political 31, equip 19, fetch 12)
  and 77 at a bleed. 568 illegal intercept plays, 2.4 a match, in three of the four starters. The ledger rates both cards FULL.
- Proposed (compiler v1.9.11): `onlyDirected: true` on the mode the sentence prefixes, on the entry when it is the header. The
  intercept finder and its mirror (`_bestInterceptFor`, `_interceptStack`), the locked route (`_bestUnlockReactFor`) and the
  location locks (`_lockInterceptFor`) skip it unless `_pendDirected(p) === true`; unknown fails closed. Underbridge Stray (curated):
  the condition on the burn alone, asked by the locked-blocker chain's third rung. The precedent: Guard Dogs' `when: 'bleedAtMe'`.
- The ledger gets the sentence's class, so a card with it and no flag is PARTIAL. The same sentence with "a bleed action" (Elder
  Intervention, not in the decks) goes to the BOARD.

## 2. 6a — the block side, on existing machinery
- **2.1 The locked route** (`when: 'lockedBlock'`: Second Tradition's and Eyes of the Wild's, D-116 (4)): Sense the Savage Way
  [ANI], Sentry Signal [ani]/[tha] (the card's 1 blood), One With the Land [PRO], Deep Ecology [pro] (`payBlood: 1`; not playable
  without the blood [ANK 20210226]; it only unlocks, and the bot plays it to block, as the route's others). The route's rules hold:
  usable by a vampire already attempting to block [ANK 20181122-2], no lifting of the prey / predator / target restriction
  [ANK 20180623]. Sense the Savage Way's "Requires a vampire with capacity 7 or more": the compiler writes `req.capMin` (7 cards in
  the pool, this one in the decks) and `_entryReqOk` reads it (only `_masterHostOk` reads a capacity today). Today its [ani] +1
  intercept is played by any Path of Caine vampire, and 7 of the 12 are below 7: the same class of misread as §1.
- **2.2 One With the Land's round**: "If they block, neither combatant can strike during the first round of the resulting combat".
  The blocker's line `VAMP: One With the Land — neither combatant strikes in the first round.` (new, frozen: the play line does not
  say the mode); both bots set `_noStrikeRound` in that round (the existing flag, Immortal Grapple's): no strike's maneuver either
  [LSJ 20001126]; again after a second block of the same action [LSJ 20010814-2], never in a follow-up combat [LSJ 20010813]. A
  human opponent is told in chat.
- **2.3 Forced Confessional [dom]**: "The blocking minion gets -1 intercept" is a stealth against the minion attempting now:
  Perfect Paragon's `icpVs` (D-116 (1)) with a new who, `this` (the minion named when the card is played); `_condStealthAdjust`
  takes it off for a later blocker.
- **2.4 True Love's Face [OBF][PRE]**: the block fails (`cancelBlock`, Elder Impersonation's), and its counter: "The blocking
  minion's controller can burn 1 pool to cancel this card as it is played". A blocking bot pays when the block is worth more than a
  pool to it (a prior: the bleed it stops is 2 or more, or the action is a rush at my vampire), with the pool rule's floor; its
  line `NAME burns 1 pool to cancel True Love's Face.` (new); the card is cancelled and the block stands. A human actor's card
  gets the same answer. A bot's card against a human blocker waits for the human's counter (a rider): until then the bot does not
  play it there.
- **2.5 Night Terrors**: "Reduce the acting minion's stealth to 0. (The acting minion can still increase their stealth.)", both modes
  (the superior's "As above"); 1 blood. A blocking bot plays it when the actor's stealth is above its best intercept and the block
  is worth the card and the blood (a prior); after the play line, `VAMP: Night Terrors — ACTOR's stealth is 0.` (new; not a
  `plays` shape, which `L12.actObs` would read as a card name); the actor's bot
  takes its stealth off the counter (`_bumpTallyA`) and may add stealth again (its second look). A human's card against a bot actor
  gets the same reset. A bot's card against a human actor is a rider (the counter's stealth is the human's to set). The superior's
  strike (combat ends, the combat from that block only [LSJ 20010813]) is a granted strike: it goes with the grants round. Today
  the mode exists but is never played (`_bestCombatEndFor` reads combat cards only: 0 of 12 in the smoke).

### 9.2 Absolute Tyranny [POT][PRE] (pathpower 3)
'Only usable during the polling step of a political action. [pot][pre] +3 votes. [POT][PRE] Vampires who do not follow the Path of
Power and the Inner Voice get −1 vote.' A vampire's net negative votes count 0 [ANK 20251125]: the cut is one vote per voting
vampire off the Path. A political action card's vote and the Edge's belong to the Methuselah (rulebook 6.3.3.1) and are not cut.
