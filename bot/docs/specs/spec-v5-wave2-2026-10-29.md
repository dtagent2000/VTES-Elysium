# V5 wave 2 — the combat's first steps: before range, range and press (29 Oct 2026, bot v0.6.214)

Johan's decision (RULINGS D-102, 29 Oct, Claude's recommendation taken): the class map's top two rows are curation queues, not
effect classes, so the first real class is maneuver + press; wave 2 takes the combat's first steps — before range, range and press
— with Flash, Resist Earth's Grasp, Slam [POT], Hunger of Marduk, Rage of Apedemak, Torn Signpost, Form of the Wolf, Monstrous Form
[dom][pro] and Walk of Flame (the round number), and gives each V5 deck a combat posture. This page is the wave's canon: the readings
with their sources (§1; RULINGS R-41), the protocol on the wire (§2), the decisions (§3; D-103: Claude's design, not Johan's
rulings), what v1 leaves out (§4; BOARD) and the tests and the registered prediction (§5).

## 1. The readings
- **Determine range** (the rulebook, combat step 2): range is close unless a maneuver changes it; the acting minion may maneuver
  first, the other may answer with a maneuver of its own, and so on, until one of them declines; the range is where the last
  maneuver left it. A maneuver goes either way.
- **A strike card with an optional maneuver** (Slam [POT]: 'with 1 optional maneuver, only usable to get to close range'): the
  maneuver cannot be used if the strike cannot be [LSJ 20021028] — the card is played in the range step and its strike is that
  round's strike (so no dodge, no combat ends, no other strike that round). The rulebook (combat step 2): a minion that uses the
  maneuver from a strike card or a weapon has chosen its strike and cannot use a second strike card or weapon to maneuver again that
  round. When a later maneuver takes the range to long, a hand strike cannot be used there and the minion is bound to it: no strike
  at all that round (the shape of the gun's ruling [TOM 19951217], read by analogy — no ruling says it directly; §5's grades).
- **A bundled optional press** (Flash [CEL], Resist Earth's Grasp [cel] as a maneuver, Form of the Wolf [PRO]) can be used only in
  the round the card was played [TOM 19960521] (Form of the Wolf's V5 text says 'this combat'; the ruling KRCG attaches to its [PRO]
  says this round — the narrower use, never illegal either way; §5's grades).
- **Soul Burn** [tha] 1R / [THA] 2R: its damage cannot be prevented by cards requiring Fortitude [LSJ 20050628]; the opposing
  vampire's weapon strikes inflict no damage that round, except after a dodge or with a first-strike weapon [LSJ 20040928].
- **Before range** ('only usable before range is determined'): Torn Signpost sets the base strength this combat (2, [POT] 3) and can
  lower a higher one [PIB 20130303]; any +X strength adds to it [LSJ 20100813]. Form of the Wolf (+1 strength, one each combat),
  Monstrous Form [dom][pro] (+1 strength, one each round), Rage of Apedemak (+1 / [ANI] or [POT] +2 on hand strikes this combat, one
  each combat; its +1 hand size is not modelled). Hunger of Marduk (requires a Banu Haqim): this round the vampire can strike,
  ranged, steal 1 ([THA] 2) blood; the provided strike can be chosen and used more than once in the round [ANK 20220207].
- **The round number:** 'not usable during (on) the first round' (Walk of Flame, Riposte; compiler v1.9.5 tags the three cards
  that print it without 'on / during') — playable from round 2.

## 2. The protocol
- **Between bots the maneuvers go back and forth.** The ACTING bot speaks first as before (`maneuvers to long (CARD).` or `does not
  maneuver.`); the other bot answers as before — or, when the acting bot went long and it wants close, with `maneuvers to close
  (CARD).` (a counter). The acting bot reads the answer as before; when that answer was a maneuver that left the range where it does
  not want it and it holds another maneuver, it answers again and waits for the answer. When it lets the range stand it says
  nothing more: its before-strikes line is the next thing the other bot hears. So the answering bot meets a late counter in its
  before-strikes wait (it waits for either line after a maneuver of its own) and answers it — a counter of its own, or `does not
  maneuver.` (the acting bot waits for that answer). Every line is an existing shape (`maneuvers to close|long` is §12's client
  line and the bot's since v0.6.156; `does not maneuver.` v0.6.202's); a round in which nobody counters sends exactly the lines it
  sent before. The rows carry `counter` (my counters) and `commit` (a strike card's maneuver) only in a round that had one.
- **No strike:** `NAME gets no strike this round (committed to CARD by its maneuver; long range).` — the shape v0.6.174 sends for
  a gun's maneuver under Immortal Grapple; the other bot now reads it as the strike step's answer (nothing lands). It was never
  parsed: the other bot waited out its safety net and took the rulebook's 1.
- **Before range** has no handshake (like Carrion Crows and Wall of Filth): each bot plays its own cards; nothing there changes what
  the other bot must answer (its strike step reads my strike line). A log line per card for the table — `NAME gets a strength of 3
  this combat — Torn Signpost [POT].`, `… gets +1 strength this combat …`, `… gets +2 damage on its hand strikes this combat …`, `… can
  steal 2 blood at any range with its strike this round …` — never a `plays` line (L12.actObs). Against a human the phase walk asks
  'Anything before range?' first when the human acts, as before.
- **Against a human** the range step is unchanged: the bot maneuvers to long, never counters (BOARD).

## 3. The decisions
- **Before range — the look-ahead rule** (v0.6.205): a card is played when the strike's own scorer (`_strikePick`, no side effects)
  deals more with it this round — at close range, where every round starts, or at long range when the posture snipes and the
  maneuver to get there is in hand (`decide('before-range')`). One card at a time, each weighed on top of the last; a card that
  costs blood keeps 1 on the vampire (none of the wave's does). The pumps last the combat (`_myPump`), the steal the round.
- **Counters** (all of them under the combat mind; combatMind 0 = v0.6.213, no counters either way): to close when `_wantClose` says
  so — the chase posture; back to long when my own first decision wanted long and another maneuver is in hand. A plain maneuver
  before a strike card's: Slam [POT]'s maneuver only when no plain one is left (it binds the round's strike), and no strike card's or
  weapon's maneuver at all once the round's strike is chosen (`_strikeBound`: a Slam's or a gun's maneuver this round).
- **Guard Dogs [ANI]'s maneuver** belongs to the vampire that played the card ('if this vampire blocks'): it survives that vampire's
  successful block into the resulting combat, dies in `_resolveCombat`'s `finally`, and is no card play when used (no ash entry, no
  play row, no clone: the card went to the ash heap, rowed, at its unlock; the `maneuvers to … (Guard Dogs).` line is the event).
- **Before range with a set range:** a round whose range is already set (a superior Immortal Grapple's close next round, a `sets
  range to` line, a human's locked range) is weighed at that range alone (`_rangeFixedPeek`; the decide row's `fixed`).
- **Soul Burn's rider:** the struck bot reads the card on the strike line (`strikes with Soul Burn for N.`, the consumed strike
  event) and plays no Fortitude prevention against it (`noPreventDisc`; a human's bare `strikes for N.` names no card).
- **Taste of Vitae** does not wait for a taken line after the opponent's clean dodge (their line reports no loss; the wait sat out its
  askSecs — 20 s at the client — and the other bot's press wait ran into its safety net: the V5 smoke's Form of Mist rounds). Clean
  means no crows, no borne damage and no additional strike of mine (their dodge covered the first pair only); a taken line already
  in is read either way — the card counts the blood they lost to damage this round, whatever dealt it.
- **Postures** (D-102): chase = Brujah, Gangrel, Tzimisce, Path of Caine; snipe = Banu Haqim; the rest wave. The bot's
  `DECK_STRATEGY` keeps the embedded decks' postures; a deck file it has no manifest for carries its own `combat` (gen-precon v1.1.0
  writes and checks it).
- **Finders:** `_bestManeuverFor(vamp, dir)` (dir long by default; a mode only usable the other way is skipped; within a card the
  mode with an optional press); `_bestCommitManeuverFor(vamp, 'close')`; the strike, steal, press and maneuver finders read the round
  number; the combat-ends finder reads 'not usable on the first round' (Riposte) — not the entry-level 'before range' flag, which
  the auto tier lifted from superior-only sentences (Alpha Glint, Gemini's Mirror, Eyes of Blades).

## 4. What v1 leaves out
- **Counters against a human** (the phase walk's range step is v0.6.165's: the bot maneuvers to long, a human's maneuver is read
  after the bot decided). **Counters for wave / snipe decks** (only chase counters to close; a snipe without a ranged card keeps the
  close range it was given).
- **Maneuver / press grants from cards played outside combat** (Hunter's Mark, Fleetness, Dread Mastiff, Form of the Bat,
  Biothaumaturgic Experiment, Monstrous Form [DOM][PRO] on the vampire) — with the rush cards (D-102).
- **Amber riders:** Rage of Apedemak's +1 hand size, its 'Frenzy.' and its Path of Caine icon; Hunger of Marduk's steal in an
  additional pair (wave 1's v1 limit); Soul Burn's opposing weapons inflicting no damage; Monstrous Form's superior action.
- **Older, met on the way:** a gun's maneuver binds its strike only under Immortal Grapple (no chase deck carries a gun); the Brujah
  crypt's printed '+1 strength' (Theo Bell, Valeriya Zinovieva) is not read — under Torn Signpost [POT] their fist deals 3, not 4; a
  counter war can spend every maneuver in round 1 (a priorities question, not a rule). Found by the second review (BOARD, 'The
  weapon's maneuver'): a weapon's maneuver is never offered — the compiler writes `maneuver: true`, `_equipCombatOption` reads a
  number, so the Tremere's .44 Magnum has never maneuvered and this wave's gun halves (`_strikeBound`'s gun arm, the ash guard) never
  run; a weapon's strike sends a clone and a play row every time; the press step waits up to 1.5 s for a taken line after a clean
  dodge; Taste of Vitae's wait returns an earlier round's pending taken line at once (the clean-dodge path reads this round's only);
  `_xLossSum` sums nothing (a later taken line marks the pending one replaced: after an additional pair Taste counts the last pair).
- **Strike choice:** `_strikePick` takes a strike card over the hands whenever it holds one (v0.6.205) — a pumped fist stronger than
  an absolute strike card (Soul Burn [tha] at 1R) loses to the card, and the look-ahead then keeps the pump (no gain). A strength
  pump is also a gamble on the range: it is weighed at close, and a maneuver to long after it wastes it.
- **The opponent's pumps** are not read (their strike line carries the result); the wave decision and the dodge do not see them
  before the strike.
